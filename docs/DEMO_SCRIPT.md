# Ten-minute sales demo

1. Open `/sign-in` and enter the Industrial Distributor Demo.
2. On the command center, frame the story: seven urgent actions and $184,200 at risk.
3. Open the HX-440 stockout. Show days of supply, linked PO, confidence, and estimated revenue impact.
4. Open Forecasting. Show actual versus selected model, confidence band, backtest WAPE, reorder point, and safety stock.
5. Return to the exception and create an assigned task. Open Action Tracker to show ownership.
6. Open Supplier Scorecards and compare Northeast Castings with Keystone Components.
7. Open AI Briefings. Point out linked exception and entity citations and the “no external data used” guardrail.
8. Open Reports and export the Executive Operating Brief.
9. Open Feedback and show feature, bug, and bad-recommendation capture.
10. Open Admin and show plan entitlements, feature flags, and white-label readiness.

Close with: “The paid pilot starts with four exports, one site, and a measurable operational target. In 30 days we deliver an ROI closeout; the full $2,500 is credited if you convert.”

## Five-minute Freight Ops demo

1. Open `/app/freight` and frame revenue, cost, margin, service, quote turnaround, and receivables together.
2. Open the tender board and select the Air Freight tender to show mode-specific AWB and chargeable-weight details.
3. Open Quote Desk, switch among LTL, TL, Spot Bid, and Air Freight, then show the transparent calculation trace and margin-floor approval.
4. Open Invoice Center, then Summary Analytics and Disputes & Credits to connect quoted, billed, disputed, and paid values.
5. Open the Client Portal. Compare quotes and point out that no internal cost, margin, or internal notes are present.
6. Open Freight Configuration to show rate-card ownership, workflow policy, accessorials, feature flags, and accounting-adapter readiness.

Close with: “FlowSight gives a smaller freight operation the commercial discipline of a much larger team, while giving the shipper a clean shared view of the same transaction.”

## Ten-minute freight procurement demo

1. Open `/app/freight` and frame the work queue: open opportunities, RFQs, responses, compliance gaps, and awards.
2. Open `/app/freight-opportunities`, create an urgent TL movement, and capture program, lane, dates, weight, commodity, SDDC/GFM relevance, and required documents.
3. Show the ranked carrier list. Expand a carrier to explain every match-score point and why an incomplete carrier is not award eligible.
4. Shortlist three carriers and generate copy-ready RFQs. Emphasize that FlowSight tracks the workflow without sending external email automatically.
5. Mark the RFQs sent and open Quotes & Award.
6. Enter three mock rates, transit times, fuel, accessorials, and notes.
7. Show the recommendation explanation: bid price, transit, carrier fit, government readiness, and compliance.
8. Award the movement and show rejected carrier states plus the immutable decision timeline.
9. Export the Markdown procurement summary with every carrier considered and the human rationale.
10. Return to Procurement Command and show the work moving through the pipeline.

Close with: “This replaces the Excel, email, SharePoint, and memory loop with one defensible freight procurement record.”
